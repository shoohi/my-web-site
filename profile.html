CURRENT RESEARCH

Learning and memory in neural networks

How do small recurrent networks learn, retain information, and use it over time?

My current research explores Hebbian plasticity and temporal state in small recurrent neural networks. I compare local learning rules with backpropagation through time to investigate how connection updates and network dynamics contribute to memory.

This is ongoing experimental work: I focus on separating what the experiments demonstrate from hypotheses that still need testing.

From sound to searchable features

An end-to-end pipeline for classifying sound effects across six categories. Acoustic features - including MFCCs, spectral features, and zero-crossing rate - turn audio into a representation a model can learn from.

Classification meets discovery

Random Forest classification pairs with cosine-similarity retrieval. A Streamlit interface lets users upload a sound, predict its category, and find related samples.
